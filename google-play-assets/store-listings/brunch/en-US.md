# Brunch 365 - en-US

## Short description

365 brunch recipes with photos, precise quantities and six languages.

## Full description

Brunch 365 is a bright, easy-to-use collection of 365 carefully selected brunch recipes from around the world.

Explore practical everyday ideas and international favourites, including eggs, porridge, pancakes, toast, baked dishes, bowls, legumes and more. Every recipe includes its own photo, precise ingredient quantities and clear preparation steps.

Search by name or ingredient, use quick category filters and save recipes to your favourites. Share a direct link to any recipe and change the interface language at any time.

Brunch 365 supports English, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Bulgarian. No account is required and the app contains no advertising.
